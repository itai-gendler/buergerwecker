# Bürgerwecker

Bürgerwecker watches appointment availability on official German municipal booking sites. Residents choose a service, offices and time preferences, receive email or push notifications when matching appointments appear, and complete any booking themselves. The website and iPhone/Android app serve German and English readers.

## Intent

“Free email notifications for free Amt appointments in German cities.” Without it, in the project's words, “appointments go to whoever hits F5 all day or pays a slot reseller. This tool reserves nothing: a free slot stays on the city's page for everyone, you just don't have to refresh it all day.” It is an independent service, not affiliated with any city, and it never books on anyone's behalf.
